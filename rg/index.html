SUPPORTED FORMATS AND ALIASES

Round labels include 03:, Q03:, Round 3:, and Question 3:. Difficulty can be 1:, Difficulty 1:, Level: easy, or a tier name. Use bullets, Option A:, or Answer: for responses; mark correct choices with Correct: B or “correct” beside an option. “Add”, “plus”, or “append” in a tier note adds choices to the previous tier. Without an add marker, that tier replaces its choices. Skipped difficulty numbers inherit the previous tier. UTF-8 text, JSON, CSV, TSV, headerless Q/A rows, and simple question - answer lines are accepted.
