=== FTL themed comprehensive Cursor Set ===

By: Xeridan (http://www.rw-designer.com/user/89401) hdetton@gmail.com

Download: http://www.rw-designer.com/cursor-set/ftl-themed-comprehensive

Author's description:

Here are all of the FTL themed cursors I've made today. I decided to make a set to go along with my FTL themed desktop and rainmeter setup I'm doing to personalize my PC! In case you don't know what FTL is, it's an awesome space themed adventure/strategy/ship management game called FTL: Faster than light.

Here's a link:
https://subsetgames.com/ftl.html#_ 

I took the four basic pointers from the game files, but everything else is my own hard work. I also had to change everything from PNGs and fix all sorts of issues related to colors. I custom drew the arrows, targets, unavailable symbol, and pen (burst laser) from scratch.

Here's a forum post on subset games where I share the desktop stuff: http://subsetgames.com/forum/viewtopic.php?f=4&t=36181

P.S. Making a small rotating gear look good is really really hard, and I could only make 2 frames!!
