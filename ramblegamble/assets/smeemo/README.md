# Smeemo red-room presentation assets

Original procedurally authored artwork for this project, with no external asset license dependencies. The twelve 256x384 RGBA card stain variations combine paper grain, dried blood, drops, drips and scratches; the red card-back is a recolor of the existing project card-back. `fog.png` is a multi-octave tileable haze texture. `apparitions.mp4` is an offline depth-buffered rasterization with smooth surface normals, material pitting and directional key/rim lighting of six moving 3D prop groups (two skulls, two spiked rings and two sets of chain links), 960x540, 120 frames, 12 seconds, exact 10 FPS, H.264, no audio. The assets are stylized procedural props, not photographic scans. No third-party runtime rendering library is required.

The video is a single lazy silent compositing layer, under cards and above the room, hidden/paused on departure, lock, freecam, pause, Dice, visibility changes and reduced-motion preference. The red grade, fog, and stains do not alter card outcome, scoring or persistence. The card backs retain the original pattern recolored to charcoal red.

References for design and architecture: American Society of Cinematographers, [Terror Through Lighting](https://theasc.com/article/terror-through-lighting/); OBS [Track Matte Stinger Transitions](https://obsproject.com/kb/track-matte-stinger-transitions); MDN [steps()](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Values/easing-function/steps), [Autoplay guide](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Media/Guides/Autoplay), [animation performance](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Performance/Guides/Animation_performance_and_frame_rate); W3C [WCAG 2.1, 2.3.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/#three-flashes-or-below-threshold). Animated lighting is localized and the existing bright-strike gate is retained; no deliberate 10 Hz full-screen flashes.
