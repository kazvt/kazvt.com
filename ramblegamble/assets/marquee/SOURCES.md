# Ramble Gamble marquee menu clip

`ramble-gamble-marquee.mp4` combines the supplied `animation-silent.mp4` with the supplied cue timing and recorded Foley samples. It is a 4.5-second, 1152 × 576 H.264/AAC fallback clip intended to play once when the pause menu opens. The gain was raised modestly for browser playback; the measured encoded peak is approximately -2.9 dBFS. `ramble-gamble-marquee-alpha.webm` is its VP8/Opus version with the dark presentation matte keyed to alpha for browsers that decode alpha video. The page tests the decoded frame and uses a screen-blend fallback if the video is opaque.

The ZIP identifies the recordings as by rubberduck, from OpenGameArt's [100 CC0 SFX pack](https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx), under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The sound cues and sample metadata are in the supplied `scene.json`, `foley-cues.csv`, and `foley-sources.json`.

`ramble-gamble-marquee-alpha.webm` and `ramble-gamble-marquee-poster.png` use the opaque MP4's dark `#141228` presentation matte as the transparency key. This keeps the pale logo-glyph faces opaque; keying the glyph color made the sky show through them and made the letters appear dark purple. `ramble-gamble-marquee-first-frame.png` is the transparent opening frame used while the video becomes ready, avoiding a flash of the finished logo before its entrance animation. The transparent video and images reveal the existing scene sky; browsers without video alpha use the screen-blend fallback so the matte does not simply become a solid panel.
