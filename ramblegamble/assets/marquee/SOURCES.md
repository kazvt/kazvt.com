# Ramble Gamble marquee menu clip

`ramble-gamble-marquee.mp4` combines the supplied `animation-silent.mp4` with the supplied cue timing and recorded Foley samples. It is a 4.5-second, 1152 × 576 H.264/AAC clip intended to play once when the pause menu opens. The gain was raised modestly for browser playback; the measured encoded peak is approximately -2.9 dBFS.

The ZIP identifies the recordings as by rubberduck, from OpenGameArt's [100 CC0 SFX pack](https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx), under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The sound cues and sample metadata are in the supplied `scene.json`, `foley-cues.csv`, and `foley-sources.json`.

The poster is a still frame extracted from the supplied animation. The video uses a screen blend over the existing scene sky so the dark animation matte does not replace the skybox.
