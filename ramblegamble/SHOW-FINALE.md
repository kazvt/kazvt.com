# READY and end credits

Open the normal menu with GO or Escape, then choose **END CREDITS** next to CONTROLS. The credits do not change any scores, rounds or online-room state. The existing menu marquee/loop pauses while the modal is open and resumes from its prior position when closed. The same owner-supplied final theme is played once at 20% volume, following the shared sound preference. The credits play automatically with no playback buttons or progress bar; Back to Menu and Escape work; host lock or a new game entry dismisses credits. Hidden or suspended pages pause rather than skip through content.

The 198.288-second recording is the original final MP3, unmodified. The crawl uses its real playback clock, with a five-second opening card, perspective-scrolling gold credits from 5–188 seconds, and the closing title through the end of the recording. The closing title remains visible until Back to Menu or Escape; reopening END CREDITS starts from the beginning. If media is unavailable, playback is rejected, or the audio clock stalls for ten seconds, the remaining crawl uses a foreground elapsed-time fallback without audio. Reduced-motion mode gives the full readable credit list in a manually scrollable view.

Credit text lives in `rg/index.html` in `.rg-credits-track`; the supplied credit order, aliases, links shown as plain text, and jokes are retained. The owner-supplied line about bloody hands/real footage is not a verified provenance statement; see the actual exterior-hands source manifest for asset provenance. No new photos, music, or external runtime requests are introduced.

The void and starfield use bounded CSS layers and static noise. The credits use a perspective text plane rather than per-character DOM animation or a new WebGL pass. Reference: [MDN CSS perspective](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/perspective), [MDN transforms](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Guides/Transforms), [MDN media currentTime](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/HTMLMediaElement/currentTime), [MDN reduced motion](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/@media/prefers-reduced-motion). The new menu track preserves the existing approximate cue alignment (see MENU-KARAOKE.md).
