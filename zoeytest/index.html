A NOTE TO EVERYONE IN ZOEY'S ORBIT

Hi everyone! I've been thinking about making a little interactive webpage to show Zoey just how loved she is by everyone here. The hope is that it becomes something she can always return to when she needs that reminder.

If you'd like to be part of it, for now I'd love two little things:

01

A fugi/chibi of yourself, or any other simple little representation of you. A transparent background is lovely if possible.

02

Three short voice recordings saying something loving in three different ways. For example: “We love you, Zoey.” “We love you so much, Zoey.” “Zoey, you mean so much to us.” Make the words and delivery your own.

The little idea: Zoey hovers over someone's character and one of their messages plays, cycling through their three recordings. On a phone, she'd tap instead.

There may be more ideas later, like a song everyone can add vocals to. We're still figuring out what's realistic, so these two things are all for now.

✦

One more important side quest: please write Zoey a message in the Kudoboard too. This little galaxy is extra, not a replacement.
