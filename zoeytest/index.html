THE CREW'S MAILBOX - REQUEST 001♡

A NOTE FOR EVERYONE IN ZOEY'S ORBIT

TWO LITTLE THINGS, PLEASE

Hi everyone! I've been thinking of making a little interactive webpage for Zoey to show her just how loved she is by everyone here. The idea is still developing, but for now I'd love to ask everyone for two things:

01

A TINY PORTRAIT OF YOU

A fugi/chibi, or any simple little representation of yourself

Transparent background preferred, so your character can float neatly in the galaxy. Your own simple drawing or avatar is perfect.

PNG with transparency is handy, but the important part is that it feels like you.

02

THREE TINY VOICE NOTES

Three short recordings, each saying something loving in a different way

For example:

  • “We love you, Zoey.”
  • “We love you so much, Zoey.”
  • “Zoey, you mean so much to us.”

Feel free to change the wording or delivery however you'd like. The idea is to let Zoey hover over your character and hear one of your little messages cycle through.

POSSIBLE BONUS LEVEL - STILL JUST AN IDEA

There may be more little requests as the page develops, including a song that everyone could contribute vocals to, if we can make that realistic with the time we have. For now, these two things are all we're asking.

This first draft has no public upload form. If you're contributing, send your files to the person who shared this request.
