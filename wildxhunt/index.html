Create hunter 1 of 4

Each hunter draws five independent cards. Assign four to Health, Armor, Speed, and Weapon; the leftover becomes free shared quarry. Any card can appear on every draw, including repeats.

Weapon balance

Set range; the remainder becomes damage.

Town

Bounties are encounters. Each notice is one complete fight with a fixed target roster. Targets arrive through the rift in waves; the party does not return to camp until the encounter is cleared or abandoned.

Health, exhaustion, and temporary speed penalties recover in town.

Quarry market

Sell one quarry card for gold equal to its card value. Selling a card never removes that result from future rift draws.

Camp

Tracking

Reveal one non-health stat for free. Spend shared quarry for another clue.

Combat

You control all four hunters through one bounty encounter. Each target arrives as a rift wave; surviving hunter states carry into the next wave.
