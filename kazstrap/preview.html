ARCADE CONTROL STUDY

Arcade cabinet buttons

The face layers a fading color-tinted sheen over its shaded enamel, with fine light and dark inset rims. Hover lifts the button slightly; pressing reverses the inner rims and settles it into the same shadow stack.

Arcade enamel face

A restrained top sheen and color-derived face gradient build the inner surface without adding thickness to the outer rim.

Flat color face

The same inset rims and interaction states with the surface gradients removed.
