Radio control GIF sources

The matching 24x24 transport and interface GIFs are from Princeton's GrowingTree
media-control set:
https://algs4.cs.princeton.edu/GrowingTree/edu/princeton/cs/algs4/growingtree/framework/

The animated speaker GIF is the archived GeoCities asset speaker1.gif from
Eureka Enterprises 6057, captured by the Internet Archive on 2009-10-26:
https://web.archive.org/web/20091026124217/http://geocities.com/Eureka/Enterprises/6057/speaker1.gif

The speaker asset's original license was not stated in the archive record.
