Made by me, handwriting was by KittyKata12#2022, and had some help from him making the normal select and waiting in background not so Janky, i recolored the resizes orange from this set: http://www.rw-designer.com/cursor-set/spa-by-piggeh ,and ignore the previews for normal select and waiting in background, it doesn't do that when you use the cursor, so i have NO idea why it does it in the preview, and sorry some of them look blurry, thing is about windows cursors is... they automatically resize them to 32x32 pixels, but nonetheless i hope you enjoy. and i used this spritesheet: https://www.spriters-resource.com/neo_geo_pocket/sonicpocketadventure/sheet/5971/ and the other sprites i got from the sonic pocket winter source code. don't expect a Sonic Pocket Adventure - Knuckles Cursors, because there aren't enough sprites. but i MIGHT make some stella the cat cursors soon tho!
